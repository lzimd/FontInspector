OpenType 1.9.1 incorporates revisions in a preliminary working draft of the 5<sup>th</sup> edition of the ISO/IEC 14496-22 “Open Font Format” standard. Current editions and amendments of ISO/IEC 14496-22 are freely available for download from the [ISO website](http://standards.iso.org/ittf/PubliclyAvailableStandards/).

Updates to this specification are noted in the [change log](changes.md).
